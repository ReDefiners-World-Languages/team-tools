Field notes & insights

What's working

  • The Feb 12 teacher-farewell Reel ("You can take the teacher out of ReDefiners\u2026") is the strongest post in the tracked history \u2014 855 views, 50 likes, and a 13.6% engagement rate, well ahead of anything else in the library.
  • Personal-story and mythbusting formats consistently outperform promotional posts on engagement rate \u2014 the Super Bowl Spanish post and the Academic Achievers recap both cleared 12%.
  • TikTok's oldest videos (Sept 2024) still pull the platform's best view counts \u2014 the tongue-twister challenge hit 1,507 views, more than 3x the account's recent uploads.

Where to improve

  • Facebook views are estimated until a native view-count field is available from the platform \u2014 treat FB engagement figures as directional rather than exact.
  • TikTok posting has slowed sharply since early 2025 \u2014 only a handful of uploads in the last 12 months versus a steady weekly cadence in 2024, and several recent videos show 0 views.
  • There's a real posting gap between Aug 2 and Aug 21 on Instagram/Facebook \u2014 worth confirming August's calendar is fully scheduled.
