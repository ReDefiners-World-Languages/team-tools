Field notes & insights

What's working

  • Our Ramadan reflections post pulled an 18.3% engagement rate on Instagram, the strongest result across all six platforms this year.
  • X is our steadiest channel by far. We're posting there every 2.8 days on average, more often than anywhere else.
  • YouTube gets the fewest posts but the biggest individual wins. The myth-busting explainer hit 1,844 views, more than any single piece of content on any other platform.

Where to improve

  • TikTok's average engagement rate sits at 3.2%, the lowest of the six platforms. Shorter hooks or trending audio are worth testing before writing the format off.
  • YouTube videos go up every 21 days on average, with one gap stretching to 26. A lighter, more frequent format could close that gap without adding much production time.
  • Facebook's top engagement post hit 37.5%, but that came from a small view count. Treat percentage spikes on low-reach posts as a caution, not a trend, until reach grows.
