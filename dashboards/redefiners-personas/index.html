Version notes

v4, down from 9 personas in v2

What changed in v4

  • Seven personas, down from eight in v3 and nine in v2.
  • Educators & School Partners merged into Community Partners & Advocates. Both described the same behavior: a third party deciding whether to host, refer, or spread the word, for free, with no transaction attached.
  • Employers & Workforce Training Buyers stayed separate. It's the one persona in this set with a real revenue line and an actual targeting brief behind it.
  • Donors split into two: Individual Donors and Institutional & Grant Funders. The content and the compliance requirements genuinely diverge.
  • Priority Community Neighbor added. It's the only persona serving the Awareness priority on its own terms, instead of borrowing an enrollment persona that doesn't quite fit.

Open follow-up

  • The FY26-27 Marketing Plan's Section 3 priority table still lists six personas under the old names.
  • It needs updating to: Individual Donors and Institutional & Grant Funders in place of the single Donor row.
  • Priority Community Neighbor added, leading the Awareness priority.
  • Community Partners & Advocates absorbing the old, separate Educators & School Partners row.

Flags to watch

  • Employers persona: no defined pain points yet for Property Management & Landscaping or Food Service & Manufacturing. Hold off on content for those verticals until that gap is filled.
  • Institutional & Grant Funders: any "Know What Funds Us" content needs Executive Director sign-off before publishing, and has to stay nonpartisan.
  • Global Explorers content aimed at parents carries no enrollment CTA. It runs on school rosters, not parent registration.
