Color Palette

Anchor Identity
ReDefiners Navy#00303F Primary backgrounds, logos, headers, professional materials

Navy anchors both ramps below. It's never a step within either one, it's the constant text, logo, and header color that sits on top of every neutral background and pairs with every accent color.

Usage rule: Amber, Marigold, Orange, and Ember combined should stay under 50% of any composition, not 50% each. Always pair an accent fill with Navy text or icons on top. Never use white text on these colors, and never use these colors as text on a white background — Amber and Orange pass WCAG 2.2 AA against Navy (8.6:1 and 5.2:1) but fail against White (1.6:1 and 2.7:1), and Ember only clears the large-text/UI threshold, never normal body text.
Neutral Ramp — Backgrounds
White#FFFFFF Primary text, negative space, digital UI backgrounds
Ice White#FAFCFF Default page and body background
Cloud White#F5FBFF Gradient stops, soft section transitions
Ocean Mist#DFF0EE Subtle backgrounds, data viz, soft accents

White, Ice White, Cloud White, and Ocean Mist form one neutral ramp, lightest to closest-to-mist, in that order. Reach for whichever step sits closest to the mood you need instead of guessing between them. Soft Cream (#FAF5E6) still has a place for print materials and warm secondary backgrounds, but it sits outside this core ramp, so use it deliberately rather than as a default.

Accent Ramp — Social & Campaigns
Vibrant Amber#FFC100 Secondary highlight only: badges, underlines, decorative accents. Not used on clickable elements or buttons.
Marigold#FF9A00 Hover states, secondary CTAs
Energetic Orange#FF7400 Primary action color: CTAs, buttons, urgent announcements, program launches. Pair with Navy text or icons, never white.
Ember#FF4D00 Pressed/active button states, urgent alert fills, dark gradient stops.

Amber, Marigold, Orange, and Ember form one accent ramp, lightest to darkest, in that order. Reach for whichever step sits closest to the moment you're designing for instead of guessing between them.

Typography System

Montserrat carries the brand. PT Serif shows up only when we want something to feel considered, like a testimonial or a mission line. Never mix in a third typeface.

"ReDefiners changed how my child sees the world. Language learning opened doors we never imagined."

Typography Best Practices
  • Maintain 1.5x line height for body text for scroll-friendly reading
  • Never use more than two typefaces in a single composition
  • For video, add subtle motion to headline text: a fade-in or slide-up
  • Always use high-contrast text and background pairings

Use the Primary Official Logo everywhere you have room for it: headers, print materials, email, presentations, event signage, social posts. Logo Symbols — Secondary Use Only

Reach for a symbol only when there's no room for the full logo: favicons, social profile photos, app icons, or a small watermark in a corner. At that size the tagline stops being legible anyway, so the symbol carries the mark on its own. Never swap a symbol in for the full logo just because it looks cleaner.

Clear Space Rule: Visual Guide

Maintain empty space equal to the height of the capital "W" in "World Languages" around all sides of the logo. We call that height X for the rest of this section.

Step 1

Take the capital W from “World Languages” in the logo's tagline. Its cap height is your unit.

Step 2

Use that same W as the minimum gap on all four sides of the full logo lockup. The hatched zone stays empty: no text, no photo, no other logo.

Correct
ENROLL TODAY

The CTA button sits a full clear space away. The logo has room to breathe.

Incorrect
ENROLL TODAY

The CTA button crowds right up against the logo, well inside the clear space zone.

Pro tip: placing the logo over a photo or busy background? Drop a solid Navy or White rectangle behind it first, then apply the clear space rule to the rectangle's edges instead of the photo.
Logo Don'ts (FY26-27 Enforcement)
  • No stretching, rotating, or recoloring outside the approved palette
  • No drop shadows, gradients, outlines, or filters on the logo
  • No placing over low-contrast imagery without a solid background box
  • No adding text, icons, or slogans inside the clear space zone

Brand Voice & Messaging

We sound like people who actually believe language changes lives, not like a brochure. Warm, direct, and specific beats clever every time.

ToneWhat It Sounds LikeExample
InspiringHopeful, forward-looking"Every word learned opens a new door."
InclusiveWelcoming, barrier-free"Classes for every age, background, and budget."
EmpoweringAction-driven, confident"You belong here. Start your journey today."
CommunityCollaborative, story-driven"Join families across Tampa learning together."
Language Principles
  • Lead with benefits: "Gain confidence," "Connect globally," "Stand out academically"
  • Highlight accessibility: "Free and low-cost options," "No prior experience needed"
  • Showcase impact: real student voices, short video testimonials, before-and-after stories
  • Avoid jargon: say "language classes," not "proficiency-based immersion programming"

FY26-27 Messaging Framework: AIDA+ Model

Attention: bold headline in Montserrat Black with a Vibrant Amber accent
Interest: a quick benefit-driven subhead and a diverse student visual
Desire: social proof, a testimonial, an enrollment stat, a partner logo
Action: a clear CTA button in Energetic Orange with Navy text: "Reserve Your Spot"
+ Follow-up: "Share your journey #ReDefinersWL" for user-generated content

Digital-First Applications

Social Media Template Strategy

Social still gets the boldest treatment in our system, but the two accent colors don't share a job here. Orange carries the bold, full backgrounds, since it's our primary action color. Amber stays in its lane as a decorative accent, never the dominant color and never anything clickable. Email and web stay calmer by comparison, using Orange only for buttons and small highlights.

PlatformVisual StrategyColor Priority
IG Reels / TikTokVertical video, quick cuts, student highlightsOrange background, Navy text and logo, Amber accent line
Instagram FeedCarousel stories, before/after journeysOrange background blocks, Navy text, Navy CTA button
FacebookCommunity events, parent testimonialsCream background, Navy text, Amber accent block behind headlines
LinkedInImpact stats, partner spotlightsNavy background, White text, Amber stat highlights
YouTube Shorts15-second success clips, teacher introsOrange background, Amber accent line transition
Email & Web UI Guidelines
  • Primary buttons: Orange fill with Navy text, 8px border-radius
  • Secondary buttons: Navy outline, white fill
  • Hover states: darken accent colors by 10%, never lighten
  • Loading and transitions: a subtle Amber pulse at 30% opacity
Video export settings: 1080×1920 (9:16) for vertical, 1920×1080 (16:9) for horizontal. Always burn in closed captions. Max file size: 500MB.
